A keyboard that feels native and keeps your typing to itself.

iBoard is a free, open-source (Apache-2.0) keyboard for iPhone running iOS 27. It looks and behaves like the Apple keyboard on day one, then quietly gets better.

  • Learns your words on your device only. Long-press a suggestion to forget it.
  • Swipe typing that uses your personal dictionary.
  • Hinglish: romanized Hindi and English in the same session, no layout switch.
  • Verification codes offered as a chip, using a Shortcuts automation you create. iBoard does not read your messages.
  • Gestures: spacebar cursor, backspace swipe-to-delete with preview, key flicks for numbers and symbols.
  • Themes, emoji search, optional clipboard history, and iPhone Duo layouts.

Private by construction

The keyboard never uses the network. No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no data collection. It works fully without Full Access. Read the privacy policy.

Free and open

No subscriptions and no paywall. The code and the bundled language data licences are public in the repository.
