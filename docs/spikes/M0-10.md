# M0-10 Spike: iPhone Duo keyboard (PARTIAL)

Status: mostly NOT TESTED. Automation could not drive input on the Duo simulator, so the iBoard extension was never shown.

## Question
Extension frame and traits on outer/inner display, rotation and fold transitions, and whether
`UIHingeInteraction` / `reservedRegions` reach the extension (P-14). System keyboard screenshots per pose.

## Method
- Cloned the "iPhone Duo" sim (iOS 27.1), booted, installed a throwaway build of iBoard whose extension displayed
  and `os_log`-ged its frame, traits, `reservedRegions(kind:.division/.occlusion, .includeInactive)` and a
  `UIHingeInteraction` callback (layout data only, no typed text). The host app focused a text field on launch.
- Driving input: `axe describe-ui` fails on this sim ("Timed out creating the simulator remote automation session").
  `axe tap` (default, `simulator` and `physical` styles) reports success but had no visible effect in Safari
  (URL bar, close button). `simctl` has no rotate, fold or pose option (`simctl ui` only exposes appearance etc.).
  Simulator.app / Device Hub were not running, so no GUI pose controls were reachable from this session.
- Screenshots: `simctl io <id> enumerate` lists two class-0 displays; `screenshot --display=<port UUID>` captures each.

## Conclusions
- P-14 remains unverified here. Do not change it.
- Display sizes in P-13 are confirmed for both framebuffers.
- Blocker for agents: need either working `axe` on the Duo sim (describe-ui fails) or Device Hub / Simulator GUI
  pose controls. Suggest the owner run this spike by hand, or retry once AXe supports this sim.

## Impact on ROADMAP
- P-13: sizes confirmed (outer 466x678 pt, inner 669x951 pt, scale 3).
- P-14 / R-07 / D-20: unchanged (unverified).
- M0-10 should stay open; M11 stays blocked on it.

## Facts for ROADMAP section 5
- `simctl io <udid> enumerate` shows 2 class-0 displays for the Duo sim; pass `--display=<port UUID>` to choose one; default screenshot hits the inner display (black when closed).
- `UIHingeInteraction` and `UIView.reservedRegions(kind:options:)` are iOS 27.1+ APIs; guard with `#available`.
- `axe describe-ui` times out on the Duo sim; `axe tap` is ineffective there.

## Owner session, 2026-10-27 (Device Hub, iPhone Duo simulator iOS 27.1, current iBoard build)

Screenshots (owner-labelled): `M0-10-assets/owner-inner-tent.png`, `owner-inner-landscape.png`, `owner-inner-portrait.png`, `owner-outer-landscape.png`, `owner-outer-portrait.png`.

What the screenshots show (visual inspection only):
- The iBoard extension loads and renders in all five poses; it fills the available width, sits at the bottom, with the three-slot suggestion strip.
- **No fold awareness (as P-14 predicts):** in inner landscape the keys continue across the vertical fold; there is no split and no centre gap, because the rule-based split (M11-03) is not wired into the keyboard yet.
- In outer landscape the keyboard stays clear of the camera cut-out, with a small margin.
