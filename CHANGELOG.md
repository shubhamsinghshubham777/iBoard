## [Unreleased] - 1.0.0 (in preparation)

### Added
- Native-look keyboard extension (UIKit, manual layout) with light/dark themes, shift/caps lock, delete acceleration.
- English and Hinglish language data, conservative autocorrect with undo, phrase chips, swipe typing.
- Personal learning with incognito mode, per-field rules, forget-a-word, import/export and a dictionary manager.
- Gestures: spacebar cursor, Shift + space selection, backspace swipe delete with preview, key flicks.
- One-time-code chips through a user-created Shortcuts automation, personal-info chips, clipboard history (opt-in).
- Emoji panel with English and Hindi search, theme studio, haptic and sound packs, landscape and iPhone Duo layouts.
- Companion app: onboarding, settings, insights, About with privacy, licences and data attributions.
- Opt-in iCloud sync of the personal dictionary (encrypted, app-side only).
- Privacy manifests for the app and the keyboard, original app icon (light, dark, tinted), privacy and support pages.
