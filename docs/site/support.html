Support

Get help

Open an issue at github.com/shubhamsinghshubham777/iBoard/issues. Please do not paste anything you typed or any personal text. For security or privacy problems use private vulnerability reporting on the repository's Security tab instead of a public issue.

Turn the keyboard on

  1. Open Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard.
  2. Choose iBoard.
  3. In any text field, press and hold the globe key and pick iBoard.

Full Access is optional. Without it iBoard still types, suggests, autocorrects and swipes. With it you also get haptics, clipboard history, the dictionary shown in the app, and iCloud sync. See the privacy policy.

Common questions

iBoard does not appear in some fields

iOS shows its own keyboard in password and phone-number fields, and some apps turn custom keyboards off. This is an iOS rule.

No haptics

iOS only allows keyboard haptics when Full Access is on (Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > iBoard). Also check that Settings > Sounds & Haptics > Keyboard Feedback is enabled.

Verification codes do not appear

Open the iBoard app and follow Verification codes. You create a Shortcuts automation for Message or Mail that contains the trigger text and runs the iBoard action "Capture Verification Code". Turn off "Ask Before Running". After restarting the phone, open Shortcuts once.

Forget a word

Long-press the suggestion, or open the app's Dictionary and remove it.

Reset everything

Use the Dictionary screen in the app to erase learned words and Insights to reset counters. Deleting the app removes local data. If you used iCloud sync, use "Delete iCloud data" in the app's Sync settings first.
